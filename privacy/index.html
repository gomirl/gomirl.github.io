Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · Early test version

The short version: MIRL only works if you trust it, so we keep as little as we can. Nobody sees you unless they're checked in at the same spot. Even then, they only see your avatar, handle and age. Photos are optional, checked by MIRL first, and only shown after you both say hi. We never sell your data and we don't run ads. You can delete your account any time, and when you do, your profile and chats are actually gone.

Who we are

MIRL ("we", "us") is a meet-in-real-life app based in Delray Beach, Florida. Questions about privacy: hello@gomirl.com.

MIRL is for adults 18 and older only. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

Your location

MIRL asks for your location when you tap check in or Near me. For check-in, we use it once to confirm you're within about a block of the venue. For Near me, it centers the map on your phone.

Area activity: if you've allowed location and Count me in area activity is on (it starts on; turn it off any time in the Living Room), then while MIRL is open your phone reports a rough area about 300 meters wide. We save only that rough area, with no name attached, and delete it after 60 minutes. It appears on the map only as a glow when 3 or more members are in the same area.

We don't store your exact GPS coordinates, and MIRL can't track you when the app is closed. For check-ins we keep only which venue and when.

Who sees what

  • The map: anyone signed in sees how many members are at each spot. Just a number, never who.
  • Inside a spot: members checked in at the same spot, who fit your settings and haven't been blocked, see your avatar, handle, age, how many interests you share, and whether you're with friends. They see "Open to casual" only if you've both turned it on.
  • After you both say hi: you each see the other's first name, and a chat opens.
  • Your photos: only after MIRL approves them, and only to people you've both said hi to, inside your chat. Never on the map. If you pick a photo as your avatar, members at the same spot, on the same plan, or in a chat with you see it instead of your cartoon. If you block someone, they can't see your photos.
  • Friends: if you and someone both tap Add friend in a chat, you become friends and the chat stays. If your Ghost Mode is off, you count toward the number of "friends here" on their map. It's a number, never your name. Ghost Mode is on by default.
  • Going lists: if you RSVP to a plan, other members who RSVP'd and fit your settings see your avatar, handle and age, and can say hi.
  • Your private side: shown only inside that chat, only if you've both turned on casual, and only the tags you have in common.
  • Open plans: signed-in members can see the plan name, venue and time. Invite-only plans are visible only to the host and the people who joined with the link.
  • Never shown to other members: your email, birthday, last name, sign-in photo, orientation, relationship style, or who you blocked or reported.

How long we keep things

  • Chats and messages are deleted after 14 hours with no new messages, unless you both tap Add friend.
  • Check-ins end after 3 hours or when you leave.
  • Photos are kept until you remove them or delete your account.
  • Your account is kept until you delete it.
  • The safety record (hashed email and reports filed about a member) is kept even after that member deletes their account, for as long as we need it to keep members safe.
  • Reported chats: if someone reports a chat, a private copy of that chat is kept for 30 days so we can review it, then deleted. Only MIRL can see it.
  • Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's backups for up to 30 days before it's overwritten.

What we never do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't post to your Google or Facebook account.

Services we use

We share only what's needed to run MIRL with these providers:

  • Supabase stores our database and handles sign-in (US servers)
  • Google and Meta (Facebook) handle sign-in if you choose them. Google Forms also runs the website waitlist.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla deliver phone notifications if you turn them on. They see only the alert itself, never your profile.
  • OpenStreetMap provides map images and the list of places (through the Overpass service). Photon (komoot) finds places when you type a spot name for a plan. DiceBear art and app code are delivered by jsDelivr, and Google Fonts provides fonts. Your device loads files from these providers directly, so they can see your IP address, like any website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your profile any time in the Living Room.
  • Delete your account in the Living Room, or see how to delete your data.
  • Get a copy of your data, or correct it: email hello@gomirl.com from your sign-in email address.
  • Block or report anyone with one tap. A report blocks that person for you right away. If two different members report someone on the same night, that person is hidden from everyone until we review it.

Security

Your data is protected by row-level security, which means members can only read their own profile. Everyone else's information reaches you only through the limited views described above. No system is perfect. If we ever have a breach that affects you, we'll tell you.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell members in the app before it takes effect.
