Delete your MIRL account and data

Applies to accounts created with Google or Facebook sign-in

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open gomirl.com/app and sign in.
  2. Tap Living Room.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: by email

Email hello@gomirl.com with the subject "Delete my MIRL account". Send it from the email address you signed in with, so we know the request is really from you. We'll delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in record, including your email, name and sign-in photo link
  • Your profile: handle, avatar, birthday, preferences, interests and private side
  • Your check-ins, plans you hosted, and your RSVPs
  • Your hellos, chats and messages, on both sides
  • Your blocks and the reports you filed

What we keep for safety: if other members reported you, those reports are saved with a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email, so deleting an account can't erase a record of bad behavior. If you were banned, the hashed email is kept so the account can't rejoin. The hash doesn't show your email address. It can only be used to recognize that same email if it signs up again.

Deleted data may stay in our hosting provider's backups for up to 30 days before it's overwritten. We don't restore it from backups.

Waitlist sign-ups

If you only joined the waitlist on gomirl.com, email us and we'll remove you from the list.

Facebook or Google connection

Deleting your MIRL account removes your account and profile data. To also remove MIRL from your Facebook or Google account settings:

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → MIRL → Remove
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/connections → MIRL → Delete all connections
